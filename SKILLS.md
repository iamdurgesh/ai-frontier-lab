## Evidence for progression

A skill advances through evidence, not time spent or generated output. Link a mechanism explanation, runnable implementation, predicted and reproduced failure, deliberate modification, and measured outcome. Record what was completed independently and where assistance was used. Revisit a rating when the evidence no longer supports it. A negative experimental result can demonstrate a skill without justifying technology adoption.
